What is GQL (Graph Query Language)?

GQL is the international standard ISO/IEC 39075:2024 for querying and managing property graph databases. It defines a language for graph pattern matching, data modification and graph structure definitions.

This free online book by Dominik Tomaszuk is intended for developers, database professionals, students and researchers. Read it chapter by chapter or use the guides below to explore a specific question.
